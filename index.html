Privacy Policy — Magic Mirror Video Call Notifications

This messaging service sends SMS notifications containing a video call link to a small number of personally known recipients (friends and family) who have verbally agreed to receive them.

What we collect

Only the recipient's mobile phone number, provided directly by the sender for the purpose of sending these notifications. No other personal data is collected, stored, or shared.

How it's used

Phone numbers are used solely to send a one-time video call invitation link when the sender manually initiates a call. Numbers are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.

Opt-out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
