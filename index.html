Privacy Policy — Home Assistant Automation Notifications

Business Name: Wadis Flores

This messaging service is operated by Wadis Flores (registered as Sole Proprietor Brand "MagicMirror" / "My Starter Profile" with Twilio). It sends SMS notifications from a personal Home Assistant system to a small number of personally known recipients who have verbally agreed to receive them.

What we collect

Only the recipient's mobile phone number, provided directly by the recipient during verbal enrollment, for the purpose of sending these notifications. No other personal data is collected, stored, or shared.

How it's used

Phone numbers are used solely to send automated SMS notifications — including alerts, status updates, and video call links — triggered by specific home automation events configured by the system owner. Message frequency varies depending on automation activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing of information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support of services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opt-out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help.
